Before you scan — please read

webscan-light sends real network requests to the targets you enter. Scanning systems can have consequences. Only continue if you understand and accept them.

  • Authorisation is required. Only scan systems you own or have explicit written permission to test. Unauthorised scanning is illegal in many jurisdictions.
  • Scans are noisy and get logged. Port scans, fuzzing and active checks can trip IDS/WAF alerts and appear in the target's logs.
  • Your IP can be rate-limited or banned. Aggressive scans (ports, URL fuzzer, network) may cause the target or your ISP/host to block your address. Lower workers and raise timeouts to be gentle.
  • Active tools can affect the target. XSS/SQLi/redirect checks send payloads; run them only against non-production or authorised systems.
  • Findings are indicative, not exhaustive. Version-based and heuristic checks can produce false positives/negatives — verify before acting.
