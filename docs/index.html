Why not just use a SaaS scanner?

Hosted scanners are fine until you hit the free-tier scan limit, need to point it at an internal/staging host, or don't love sending your source code and target list to a third party. webscan-light runs entirely on your own infrastructure — a laptop, a CI runner, or a small VPS — under a free, auditable, open-source licence.

Frequently asked questions

Is webscan-light really free?
Yes. It's AGPL-3.0 licensed, source-available, and free for commercial and personal use. There's no account, no scan quota, and no data leaves your machine — it runs on your own server or laptop.
Do I need to install anything to try it?
Yes, it's self-hosted: pip install webscan-light[web] or docker run ghcr.io/lewisjohnvillamor/webscan-light, then open the local web UI or use the CLI. There's no hosted SaaS version.
Is this a replacement for a full penetration test?
No. It's a light scanner — version/heuristic checks, misconfiguration detection, and safe-PoC active testing. It won't replace a manual pentest, but it catches the common, easy-to-miss issues fast and for free, continuously.
